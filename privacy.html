Privacy Policy

What mozAI collects, which parts of your work stay local, and what happens when you use AI.

Effective November 8, 2026 · Invite-only beta.

1. About mozAI

mozAI is an independent image-editing application in early beta. This notice covers the website, guest editor, invited accounts, and AI features. For privacy requests during the invite-only beta, contact the operator through the invitation email or original beta communication channel.

2. Information we process

  • Account information: email address, authentication identity, invitation status, and membership role, processed with Supabase.
  • Usage records: project identifiers, upload/save events, generation type, model, quality, timestamps, success or failure, and token/cost estimates when reported.
  • Editable project content: uploaded images, selections, masks, and prompts ordinarily remain in your browser until you choose to export an editable .moz file. Server-side usage analytics do not store full project images or prompts.
  • AI requests: when you request generation or edits, images, instructions, masks, and selected references are transmitted through mozAI's Vercel-hosted API functions to OpenAI.
  • OpenAI API key: an API key you connect is validated, encrypted on the server, and saved as a restricted encrypted record so future requests can use it. The key must be decrypted on mozAI's server to call OpenAI. It does not appear in project exports or admin analytics.
  • Technical information: essential session cookies and hosting-provider security/diagnostic data required to operate and protect the service.

3. Purposes and sharing

We use these records to sign you in, enforce access, process requested edits, show usage, diagnose failures, and protect the service. We do not sell user data or use uploads for targeted advertising. Vercel processes hosted requests, Supabase processes account records and encrypted credentials, and OpenAI processes requested AI generations. These providers have their own applicable agreements and policies.

4. Third-party AI processing

Images and prompts sent to OpenAI are subject to the relevant OpenAI API terms and data practices. We cannot promise zero provider retention, particular training settings, or confidentiality guarantees beyond the applicable provider arrangement. Do not submit proprietary client material unless you have authority to use the chosen AI service.

5. Storage, retention, and security

Editable projects are not automatically saved to a mozAI cloud project store. Refreshing your browser can erase unsaved work; downloaded .moz files stay wherever you save them. Account and usage metadata can remain while your account is active and afterward for reasonable security, operational, or legal needs. Encrypted OpenAI credentials can be removed from your account profile; clearing locally saved files is a separate action.

mozAI uses HTTPS, account-based permissions, and encryption of stored credentials, but no internet service guarantees absolute security.

6. Requests and updates

For requests concerning account access, correction, deletion, or privacy, reply to your original mozAI beta invitation or the channel through which you were invited. Some records may need to be retained for legal or security reasons. This page may change as features evolve; the effective date above will be updated.

Beta caution: mozAI is not intended as a secure repository for sensitive client work. Your own OpenAI API billing and data settings are separate from your mozAI account.
